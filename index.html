Meet our customer

Actash

A full‑stack robotic engineering and automation company building custom and standard systems across the whole product lifecycle — from design and engineering through manufacturing and deployment. Led by CEO Doruk Aktas, Actash delivers collaborative robots for safe work alongside people and industrial robots for high‑throughput production.

Actash set out to build a proprietary AI‑assisted automation and digital‑twin platform tailored to its welding workflows — removing manual robot programming and scaling adaptive operations across its portfolio.

The challenge

Every weld, programmed by hand

01

Manual bottleneck

Engineers read the CAD, found seams and taught paths point‑by‑point on the pendant.

02

No safety net

Auto‑generated programs had to be proven reachable, collision‑free and correctly oriented first.

03

Multi‑vendor fleet

FANUC, UR and KUKA controllers — output had to translate, not lock in.

04

Lean PoC scope

A tight vertical slice, proven end‑to‑end in simulation before committing hardware.

The vision

A CAD file goes in. A validated, robot‑ready program comes out.

Deterministic engineering pipelines for ingestion, geometry and path generation — an AI agent layer for job understanding and orchestration — and a digital twin that validates every program before it reaches a physical robot.
